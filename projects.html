DREAM ~ LEARN ~ ACHIEVE

Every child deserves a safe, inspiring environment where they can learn confidently, dream boldly and work toward a brighter future. From 18th to 21st September 2026, Le Shelter Foundation will undertake its project at Anyirawase D/A Junior High School in the Volta Region. The project will bring volunteers and community members together to improve the learning environment and provide essential educational support to students. For many children in underserved communities, limited educational resources and inadequate learning spaces can make it difficult to concentrate, participate and reach their full potential. This project seeks to address that challenge by creating a more welcoming and motivating environment where students feel valued, supported and encouraged to succeed. The project is not only about improving a school, it is about investing in young lives, strengthening a community and giving children a better opportunity to shape their own futures.

Give today; Volunteer with us; Together, we can build brighter futures; one child and one community at a time.
