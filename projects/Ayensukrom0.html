A New Chapter in Ayensukrom M/A School

How one outreach is transforming lives, health, and education in a rural community.

Tucked away in the heart of Samraboi, a remote cocoa farming village in Ghana’s Western Region, Ayensukrom M/A School has long stood as a symbol of perseverance amid adversity. For years, students and teachers braved crumbling classroom walls, sat on broken desks, and studied without basic learning materials-determined to pursue education against all odds. The journey to school itself, over deeply eroded, unpaved roads, was a daily struggle. Yet despite these overwhelming challenges, the spirit of this school community remained unbroken.

That resilience was recently rewarded through a gesture of compassion and solidarity. Thanks to a significant and timely donation from our dedicated partners, Ayensukrom M/A School has begun to witness a long-overdue transformation-one that has renewed hope, restored dignity, and reignited dreams marking a turning point in the school’s journey. Additionally, students received new books, writing supplies, and other essential educational resources, while teachers were equipped with improved teaching materials. This intervention has brought more than just physical changes-it has restored confidence, motivation, and a renewed sense of pride within the school. We extend our deepest gratitude to the donors whose generosity has brought lasting change to Ayensukrom M/A School. Your support has not only improved a school’s infrastructure but uplifted an entire community.

As we celebrate this incredible milestone, we invite others to join us in uplifting rural education across Ghana. The journey to equity in education is long, but with every act of giving, we take one step closer to a future where no child is left behind-no matter where they are born. We extend our heartfelt gratitude to the donors whose kindness has touched the lives of so many. Your support has not only changed a school-you have changed a community.

"This intervention changed everything for us. Our children are more motivated and we now have proper facilities to support their learning."
