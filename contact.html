Your questions, answered

How can I support a project?

You can donate towards school supplies, share your time as a volunteer or discuss an in-kind contribution. Contact our team to understand current needs and arrange your support.

How can I become a volunteer?

Tell us about your interests, skills and availability using the form. Our team can help you find a practical way to contribute.

Where does Le Shelter work?

We work with schools and communities in rural Ghana, including Asiafo Amanfro, Asutsuare, Ayensukrom, Apewosika and Anyirawase.

Can organisations partner with the foundation?

Yes. We welcome conversations about learning resources, professional skills, mentorship and community partnerships.

Can I donate school supplies?

Books, stationery, uniforms and other learning materials can help. Please contact us first to discuss current needs and arrange delivery or collection.

How can I find out about upcoming projects?

Visit Our Projects to explore the featured event and project stories. Contact us to confirm the current schedule before making travel or volunteering plans.

How do I ask about merchandise?

Browse our Shop to select items, or contact us with questions about availability, delivery and collection.

How can I stay connected?

Follow Le Shelter Foundation on our social channels for community moments and updates, or send us an email with your questions.

Le Shelter Foundation provides equal educational opportunities for children in rural Ghana.
