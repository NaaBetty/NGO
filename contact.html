Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How are donations used?

100% of donations support school supplies, infrastructure, water systems, and mentorship programs directly in our partner schools.

❓ How can I volunteer?

We welcome volunteers for school visits, mentorship, fundraising, and community engagement. Contact us to discuss opportunities.

❓ Are donations tax-deductible?

Donations may be tax-deductible depending on your country. Please retain your receipt and check with local authorities.

❓ Can organizations partner with LSF?

Yes! We welcome corporate partnerships, CSR initiatives, and institutional collaborations. Let's discuss tailored programs.

❓ How often do you revisit schools?

We conduct annual assessments and major revisits (like Sept 2025) to track progress and adapt our support based on needs.
