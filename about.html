Le Shelter Foundation is a humanitarian NGO founded in 2017 by Ruby Offeibea Adu. We believe a child's location should never limit their opportunity to learn.

Working alongside rural communities in Ghana, we provide school supplies, mentorship and practical support. Together, we help young people build confidence and imagine what comes next.
