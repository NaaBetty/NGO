Le Shelter Foundation is a humanitarian NGO founded in 2017 by Ruby Offeibea Adu. We believe a child's location should never limit their opportunity to learn.

Working alongside rural communities in Ghana, we provide school supplies, mentorship and practical support. Together, we help young people build confidence and imagine what comes next.

Learning today. Opportunity tomorrow.

Our mission is to provide equal educational opportunities for all, focusing on children in rural Ghana through learning materials, guidance and meaningful engagement.

By working alongside schools and communities, we help students build confidence, find encouragement and see new possibilities for their futures.

Every child seen. Every dream supported.

We envision a future where a child's background or location does not limit their opportunity to learn, grow and thrive.

Through inclusion, empathy and community service, we work towards stronger communities where children can pursue their potential with dignity and support.

LOCAL ACTION. A SHARED FUTURE.

Our Commitment to a Sustainable Future

Education opens doors to opportunity, dignity and stronger communities. Our mission connects with the Sustainable Development Goals through learning, inclusion, well-being and working together.

With a clear mission aligned with global development goals, we strive to be a beacon of hope for those in need while fostering dignity and empowerment.
